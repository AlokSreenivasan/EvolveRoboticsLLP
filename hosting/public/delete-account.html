Delete your Evolve app account

This page is for the Evolve mobile app published by Evolve Robotics LLP on Google Play and the App Store. It explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Evolve app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  4. If you created the account with email and password, enter your current password when asked.

Deletion through the app starts immediately and cannot be undone. You will be signed out when it completes.

How to request deletion by email

Use this if you have uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or you are a parent or guardian requesting deletion of a child’s account.

  1. Email evolveroboticsindia@gmail.com with the subject line Evolve app account deletion request.
  2. Include the email address on the Evolve account, the full name on the profile if you know it, and (optional) school name. Parents should state that they are the parent or guardian.
  3. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder or a parent/guardian, then delete the account.

Email requests are processed within 30 days of verification. Phone: +91 75939 99111 (Monday–Saturday, 10am–5pm).

Data we delete

When deletion completes we remove:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email/password or Google sign-in)
  • Your Firestore profile (name, phone, school, grade, track, birth year, parental-consent record, and related account fields)
  • Learning activity we store for your user id (including exam and quiz attempts, progress, notification preferences, and in-app notifications)
  • Push notification (FCM) tokens for your devices
  • Profile photos in Firebase Storage
  • Local session and preference caches on a device that is signed in

Data that may be kept, and for how long

  • Class forum messages: text you already posted may stay in the school/grade channel so the class conversation still makes sense, or it may be removed or anonymised during deletion.
  • Forum reports: if a message was reported, a copy of the reported text and reporter details may be kept as needed to finish review, then only as long as required for that purpose or a legal obligation.
  • Crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics): crashes already sent may be retained according to Firebase Crashlytics defaults (typically up to 90 days).
  • Legal or safety records: we may keep a limited record if required by law, to complete the deletion request, or to protect a child or other user.

We do not keep your account in order to send marketing. YouTube may retain its own playback data under Google’s privacy policy when you watched course videos; we do not control that retention.
